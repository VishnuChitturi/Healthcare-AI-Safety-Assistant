# NIH/AHA Clinical Guideline: Chest Pain Emergency
**Source:** NIH Heart Attack Warn Signs / American Heart Association
**URL:** https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart-attack

## Key Information
* Chest pain, chest pressure, tightness, or a squeezing sensation in the center of the chest is a critical warning sign of a myocardial infarction (heart attack).
* If chest pain occurs with shortness of breath, difficulty breathing, sweating, or pain radiating to the jaw, neck, back, or arm (especially left arm), it is a medical emergency.
* Immediate Action: Bypasses clinical triage. Advise the patient to call emergency services (108 in India, 911 in the USA) immediately. Do not drive to the hospital; wait for paramedics.
* Risk multipliers: History of diabetes, hypertension, elderly status, and smoking significantly increase heart attack mortality risk.
