# WHO/ADA Clinical Guideline: Diabetes & Insulin Care
**Source:** World Health Organization / American Diabetes Association Factsheets
**URL:** https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets

## Key Information
* Diabetes is a chronic metabolic disease characterized by elevated levels of blood glucose.
* Type 1 diabetes requires daily administration of insulin. Type 2 diabetes management focuses on lifestyle, diet, oral hypoglycemic agents, and sometimes insulin.
* Hyperglycemia (high blood sugar) symptoms include extreme thirst, frequent urination, fatigue, and blurry vision.
* Hypoglycemia (low blood sugar, under 70 mg/dL) is an acute danger characterized by shakiness, sweating, confusion, dizziness, and rapid heartbeat. Immediate ingestion of fast-acting sugar (e.g. fruit juice, candy, or glucose tablets) is required.
* Medical Safety warning: Insulin doses should never be adjusted without explicit instructions from a treating endocrinologist.
