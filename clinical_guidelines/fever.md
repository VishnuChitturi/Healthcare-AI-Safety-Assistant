# CDC Clinical Guideline: Fever & Infection Control
**Source:** CDC Influenza Symptom Triage Guidance
**URL:** https://www.cdc.gov/flu/symptoms/index.html

## Key Information
* Fever can be a normal immune response indicating infection (viral, bacterial, or fungal).
* Normal adult body temperature is approximately 98.6°F (37°C), but a fever is generally defined as a temperature of 100.4°F (38°C) or higher.
* Pediatric alert: A fever in infants under 3 months of age (temperature of 100.4°F/38°C or higher) is a critical emergency requiring immediate evaluation.
* Elderly alert: High or persistent fever in patients aged 65 or older represents a high risk of sepsis and requires urgent medical review.
* Standard support: Stay hydrated with water or electrolyte solutions, rest, and keep the body cool. Persistent fevers exceeding 3 days require a doctor's examination.
