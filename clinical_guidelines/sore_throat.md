# CDC Clinical Guideline: Sore Throat & Pharyngitis
**Source:** CDC Group A Strep Guidelines
**URL:** https://www.cdc.gov/groupastrep/diseases-public/strep-throat.html

## Key Information
* Most sore throats are viral; antibiotics are only effective for bacterial infections (such as Strep Throat) confirmed by a clinician.
* Do not self-treat or demand antibiotics from a physician without a rapid antigen detection test or throat culture confirming Group A Streptococcus infection.
* General supportive care includes warm salt water gargles, hydration with warm liquids, rest, and sleep.
* Pediatric alert: If the child has a sore throat accompanied by drooling, difficulty swallowing, or a muffled voice, seek immediate emergency pediatric care.
